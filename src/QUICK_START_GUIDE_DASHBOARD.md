# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Guide Dashboard

## What's New?

Your Guide Dashboard now has **4 powerful new features** organized in tabs:

### 📋 Overview Tab (Default)
- **Pending Bookings**: Accept or decline new booking requests
- **Active Bookings**: View confirmed bookings with maps
- **Notifications**: See recent activity and updates
- **Quick Stats**: Pending requests, active bookings, total earnings

### 📅 Availability Tab
- **Interactive Calendar**: Click dates to add/remove availability
- **Time Slots**: Set start and end times for each day
- **Visual Indicators**: See which days have availability
- **Easy Management**: Add or remove slots with one click

### 💰 Earnings Tab
- **Total Earnings**: All-time earnings display
- **Period Earnings**: Filter by Month/Quarter/Year
- **Payout Requests**: Request withdrawals to your bank
- **Recent Earnings**: List of recent bookings and payments
- **Analytics**: Average per booking, completed bookings count

### ⭐ Reviews Tab
- **Average Rating**: Your overall rating from tourists
- **Rating Distribution**: See breakdown of 1-5 star reviews
- **Individual Reviews**: Read what tourists say about you
- **Sorted by Recent**: Latest reviews appear first

## 🔔 Notifications Center

**New Feature**: Click the bell icon in the header to see:
- Unread notification count
- Booking requests and updates
- Tourist inquiries
- Mark as read / Delete options
- Real-time updates

## ✅ First-Time Setup

When you first log in as a guide:

1. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Fill in your details (4 steps)
   - Upload a profile picture
   - Set your hourly rate
   - Submit for verification

2. **Verification Alert**
   - You'll see a yellow alert on your dashboard
   - Your profile is under review
   - You can still manage tours while pending
   - You'll get an email when approved

3. **Start Managing**
   - Accept/decline bookings
   - Set your availability
   - Track your earnings
   - Respond to reviews

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Questions

**Q: Why is my profile pending verification?**
A: New guides need approval. Check your email for updates.

**Q: How do I change my hourly rate?**
A: Update it in your profile page.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Payouts are processed within 3-5 business days.

**Q: Can I see past earnings?**
A: Yes, use the Year filter in the Earnings tab.

**Q: How are reviews calculated?**
A: Average of all 5-star ratings from completed bookings.
